# Fullscreen UI clarity - 2026-09-27

Shop hover now shows the price, traits, role and a short ability cue. Mortem's
cue is "Slashes; every third cast cleaves and heals." Right-click opens the full
unit record, including goals, approaches, attack, ability and targeting details,
without buying the offer. Purchase cues cover every unit in the shop catalog and
use the live Blood Will Pay design document and current ability resources.

AFTER LOSS beside the resulting reserve, and shop actions say Reroll and XP
beside their prices. Secondary labels no longer inherit dimmed rail opacity;
the planning status and combat HUD use brighter text. Initial planning metrics
show a waiting message instead of zero rows, while available battle metrics use
Enemy / Close enemy and full Damage / Casts / Healing names.

cap. Empty cells have quieter edges, occupied cells retain their full border,
and pointer/focus states still identify placement targets. No cells were removed
and no combat, economy or positioning calculations changed.
